School Profile

San Pedro Relocation Center National High School

SPRCNHS is a public secondary school located at Barangay Langgam, City of San Pedro, Laguna. The Department of Education identifies it as a technical-vocational secondary school with a long history of industry-linked education.

School ID: 307907
Address: Imelda Avenue, Barangay Langgam, City of San Pedro, Laguna, Philippines.
Current School Head: Enrique R. Malimata, Principal IV
Established: 1970
Technical-Vocational Identity

Built around skills, pathways, and community needs

SPRCNHS shifted to the Strengthened Technical-Vocational Education Program in 2007 and was among schools designated to offer technical-vocational courses based on industry and community needs. The school also participated in the early Senior High School modelling program.

School History

Milestones through the years

A timeline of key moments in the development of SPRCNHS.

1966

Carmona Resettlement Project

The resettlement project relocated thousands of families, creating an urgent need for accessible schools within the community.

1970

Laguna Resettlement Community High School established

The school was established at United Bayanihan, San Pedro, Laguna, with Bartolomea E. Pedron as principal.

1972

Converted to SPRCNHS

The school became San Pedro Relocation Center National High School through Republic Act No. 6026, with Lourdes Z. Javier serving as principal.

1986

Langgam Campus created

Expansion responded to increasing enrollment and community growth.

1989

Cuyab Campus created

A second expansion added another campus to serve learners across San Pedro.

2002–2003

Landayan Campus opened

The Landayan annex opened on October 25, 2002, with operations beginning in June 2003.

2007

Strengthened Technical-Vocational Education Program

SPRCNHS changed from the Basic Education Curriculum to the Strengthened Technical-Vocational Education Program.

2009

Langgam became the Main Campus

The Langgam Campus became known as SPRCNHS Main Campus as the former old main campus was turned over to the Polytechnic University of the Philippines San Pedro Campus.

2026

New pathways continue

SPRCNHS became the first educational institution in Laguna publicly reported as authorized to offer the specialized Tech-Pro Maritime Senior High School pathway through its Sea Mariners partnership.
