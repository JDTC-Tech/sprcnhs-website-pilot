Tech-Pro Maritime

Sea Mariners Ship 106 commissioned at SPRCNHS

Fifty-four pioneering student crew members took their oath as a new maritime learning pathway took shape at SPRCNHS.

July 2–3, 2026 · SPRCNHS Website

Sea Mariners Ship 106 was formally commissioned at San Pedro Relocation Center National High School in July 2026, marking the first Sea Mariners Ship in the City of San Pedro.

Fifty-four pioneering student crew members took their oath during the commissioning. The program introduces learners to maritime discipline, leadership, environmental stewardship, teamwork, and basic naval drills.

Public partner reporting also identified SPRCNHS as the first educational institution in Laguna authorized to offer the specialized Tech-Pro Maritime Senior High School pathway.

The initiative adds a new industry-linked option to the school's technical-professional identity and expands the range of career pathways that may be explored by Senior High School learners.
