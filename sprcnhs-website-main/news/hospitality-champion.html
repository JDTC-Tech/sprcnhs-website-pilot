SPRCNHS named Overall Champion at 2026 Biñan Trimex Hospitality Challenge

Multiple first-place finishes and a strong second-place result helped SPRCNHS capture the overall title.

March 23, 2026 · SPRCNHS Website

San Pedro Relocation Center National High School was named Overall Champion in the 2026 Biñan Trimex Hospitality Challenge, a competition that tested students in practical hospitality-related skills.

Anna Crizza Mae Marca earned 1st Place in the Table Napkin Category. The Waiters Relay team composed of Anthony Alcantara, Rica Seiya K. Dimaranan, Liancy Rubio, Renzo Marte, and Imma Ballon also finished 1st.

Ma. Fatima Esureña and Ma. Michelle Gabrentina placed 2nd in Table Skirting. The combined results helped SPRCNHS secure the overall championship.

The achievement highlights the school's long-standing technical-vocational identity and the practical competencies developed through skills-based learning.
